I got tired of the same 10 minutes before every ComfyUI session on RunPod: pick a
GPU, paste the image, set the disks, add the env vars, wait, then go hunting for
the URL.

So I built a small Windows app that does it in one click.

→ Live GPU prices and availability, so you can see what's actually free right now
→ Tick the models you want and it sizes the disk for you
→ One click to open ComfyUI once the pod is ready
→ Stop or terminate from the app, so nothing keeps billing after you close the laptop

It deploys my own ComfyUI image, with LTX-2.3, Krea 2, FLUX.2 Klein and Wan 2.2
ready to pull on demand — and the workflows for each already in place.

Free and open source, installer in the repo:
github.com/BISAM20/comfyui-runpod-launcher

Built it for myself. Sharing it in case it saves someone else the same 10 minutes.
